The Poetry Room

Every poem here was written because one day, I met you.

Longing

Distance has never taught me how to love you less. It has only taught me how much of my day quietly belongs to you.

Devotion

I do not promise perfection. I promise that tomorrow, just as today, my heart will keep choosing you.

Midnight Thoughts

Sometimes I wonder how someone I once didn't know became someone I cannot imagine my life without.

Future

I dream of ordinary mornings, shared coffee, quiet evenings, and growing old beside the same smile that makes my heart race today.
